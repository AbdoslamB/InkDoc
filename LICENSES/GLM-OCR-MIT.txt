GLM-OCR model weights (zai-org/GLM-OCR), in the GGUF conversion ggml-org/GLM-OCR-GGUF
(revision 65a42de1148dbed2297e922b5dbc7d9b70c36578). Optional download; InkDoc also mirrors the
files on its own GitHub release "glm-ocr-v1".
